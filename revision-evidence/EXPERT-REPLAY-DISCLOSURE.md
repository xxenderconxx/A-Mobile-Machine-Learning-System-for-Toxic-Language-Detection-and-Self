# Expert replay availability disclosure

The expert review reported a 34.8% false-positive rate from a replay of 3,000 samples. The original replay samples and sufficient details of their source and replay procedure are unavailable to the researchers. Consequently, this result could not be independently reproduced, and its comparability with the classifier-level evaluation cannot be established.

The reported 34.8% finding is retained as historical expert evidence. It is not treated as invalid, nor do the revised application's code changes or subsequent tests establish that this false-positive rate has been resolved. Evaluations of the revised version are reported separately with their available inputs, model version, procedure and scope.

This limits conclusions about the revised application's end-to-end false-positive performance. The developer-authored sanity cases and classifier-only test-set results do not substitute for the unavailable expert replay.
